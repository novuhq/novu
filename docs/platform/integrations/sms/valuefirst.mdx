You can use the [ValueFirst](https://www.myvfirst.com/) provider to send SMS messages to your customers using the Novu Platform with a single API to create multi-channel experiences.

## Getting Started

To use the ValueFirst provider in the SMS channel, you need a ValueFirst account with your username, password, and a sender ID configured.

## Setting up ValueFirst

<Steps>
<Step title="Get your API Credentials">
1. [Sign in](https://www.myvfirst.com/) to your ValueFirst account
2. Navigate to your account settings to find your **Username** and **Password**
3. Configure a **Sender ID** (also known as Sender Name) for your SMS messages
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the Credentials in Novu">
1. Visit the [Integrations Store](https://dashboard.novu.co/integrations) on Novu
2. Click the **Add a provider** button
3. Locate **ValueFirst** and click on **Disabled** to mark it as **Active**
4. Click on the **Connect** button
5. Enter your:
   - **Username**
   - **Password**
   - **Sender ID**
6. Click **Save**
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ValueFirst uses token-based authentication. The SDK automatically obtains and caches a bearer token
  using your credentials. An entity ID and DLT template ID can be passed as custom data for
  TRAI-compliant messaging in India.
</Note>
