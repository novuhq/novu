You can use [Eazy](https://developers.eazy.im/) to send SMS messages through Novu.

## Getting started

1. Log in to your Eazy account.
2. Copy your **API Key** from the developer settings.
3. Copy the **SMS Channel Id** for the channel you want Novu to send through.

## Creating an Eazy integration with Novu

1. Visit the [Integrations](https://dashboard.novu.co/integrations?utm_campaign=docs-sms-eazy) page on Novu.
2. Click **Add a provider**.
3. Locate **Eazy** under the SMS section and click **Connect**.
4. Enter your:
   - **API Key**
   - **SMS Channel Id**
5. Confirm **Active Integration** is on. New integrations start active by default.
6. Click **Create Integration**.

You can now send SMS notifications with Eazy through Novu.
